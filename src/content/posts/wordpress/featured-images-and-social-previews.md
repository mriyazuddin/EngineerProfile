Your post can look great on your site and still look bare when someone shares it. Two small settings usually decide that: the featured image and the social preview.

A featured image is the main picture that represents a post or page. Your theme decides where it appears, often at the top of the post and on blog listings.

To add one, open the post's settings panel, find Featured image and choose a picture from your media library. Add alt text there too.

A social preview is the card that appears when someone shares your link on LinkedIn or elsewhere. WordPress doesn't fully handle these on its own. An SEO plugin or a feature from your plan usually lets you set the title, description and image.

A few habits help:

Use a consistent shape, because themes often crop images.\
Keep important details near the centre, in case the edges are cut off.\
Avoid putting text in the image that people must read.

Test the link before you share it widely. Social sites can keep an old preview for a while after you change it.
