This week, you learned how WordPress organises content and met the blocks it's built from. These decisions are simple, and they make a real difference as your site grows.

This week covered:

Posts are dated, and pages are lasting.\
Categories are broad, and tags are specific.\
The media library stores images, videos and files, and alt text describes images.\
A featured image represents a post, and your theme decides where it appears.\
Drafts, scheduling and revisions give you a safer workflow.\
Each piece of content is a block you can move and edit.
