There are dozens of blocks available, but a small handful will cover most of what you build. Learn these five first.

Paragraph: your ordinary text, and the default. Just start typing.\
Heading: section titles. Use levels in order, with H2 for main sections and H3 beneath them. Don't pick a level because of its size, because screen readers and search engines use this structure.\
Image: add a picture from your media library or upload a new one. Fill in the alt text here, in the block's settings.\
List: bullet points or numbered steps, for anything people will scan. Press Enter for each new item.\
Button: a clear call to action, like "Book a call". Add a link and keep the wording specific.

You can also convert one block into another. Select a paragraph, click its icon in the toolbar and turn it into a heading or list, keeping your text.

The slash shortcut speeds things up: type /heading or /button on a new line.

Go deeper: [Core blocks reference](https://developer.wordpress.org/block-editor/reference-guides/core-blocks/)
