Should your new content be a post or a page? They look almost identical in the editor, but they do very different jobs.

Posts are dated and time-sensitive. They appear in reverse date order, usually on a blog page, and can be organised with categories and tags. Examples include news, articles and announcements.

Pages are lasting and stand alone. They aren't part of the date-ordered feed, and they usually live in your menu. Examples include About, Contact, Services and Privacy Policy.

A quick test: would this content make sense with a date on it, as one of a series of updates? If so, it's a post. Would it stay relevant and need a permanent place on your site? That's a page.

A simple example: a shop's Returns Policy is a page. "Our new summer range has arrived" is a post.

You can have a site with no posts at all. Many small business sites are mostly pages, and that's perfectly fine.
