There are thousands of WordPress themes to choose from, and a good-looking demo tells you very little. Use this checklist before you commit.

1. Recently updated. Check when it was last updated. A theme that's been untouched for years may have compatibility or security problems.
2. Fast and light. Look for a theme that does what you need without a lot of extras. Try its demo on your phone.
3. Works on mobile. Many of your visitors will be on phones. Resize the demo and check the menu and the text.
4. Supported. Read recent reviews and support replies. Do people get answers when they ask questions?
5. Fits your plan. Choose based on the pages from your plan, rather than the stock photos in the demo. Those photos make every theme look better.

The WordPress.org theme directory is a good place to start, as themes there go through a review process. If you're buying elsewhere, buy from a reputable seller and avoid pirated copies, which can contain malware.
