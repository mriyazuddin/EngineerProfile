Before you add any content, spend five minutes in Settings.

![The General Settings screen in WordPress, showing the Site Title, Tagline, Site Language and Timezone fields](/images/posts/wp12-general.webp)

These small choices show up in more places than you'd expect, so they're worth getting right.

Go to Settings, then General. Check these:

Site Title: your site or business name. It can appear in your header, in browser tabs and in search results.

Tagline: a short line about what you do. Replace any placeholder text, or leave it blank if you don't need one.

Site Language: the language used for the admin screens and some text visitors see.

Timezone: choose your city or a matching UTC offset. If this is wrong, scheduled posts can go out at the wrong time.

Date and time format: pick what your readers expect, such as 2 November 2026.

Click Save Changes at the bottom when you're done. It's easy to forget.

WordPress.com vs self-hosted: the same settings exist on both, though the screen may be laid out differently.
