Large photos are one of the most common reasons for slow websites, and it's easy to fix before you upload. Your images all live in the media library.

Find it under Media in the admin menu. Anything you upload appears here, ready to reuse without uploading again.

Before uploading:

Resize big photos. A photo straight from a phone is usually far larger than a web page needs.\
Compress them using an image tool.\
Give files clear names, like blue-leather-bag.jpg rather than IMG_4032.jpg.

When you upload, WordPress automatically creates several smaller versions, so it can serve a suitable size for the space.

Coming soon: WordPress 7.2, due on 8 December, is set to resize and compress images in your browser before they reach your server, starting with Chrome and Edge. Cropped copies will also stay linked to their original, so you can re-adjust a crop later.

Then there's alt text, a short description of the image. Screen readers read it aloud for people who can't see the image, and it's shown if the image fails to load. Search engines use it too. Describe what matters: "Blue leather bag with a brass clasp". If an image is purely decorative, it can be left empty.
