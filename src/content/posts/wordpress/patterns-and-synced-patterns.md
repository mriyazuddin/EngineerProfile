Why build a pricing table or call-to-action section from scratch when someone has already designed one? Patterns are ready-made groups of blocks.

Open the block inserter, switch to Patterns and browse by type. Click one, and it's added to your page. Then replace the text and images with your own. Your theme usually brings its own patterns, so the design tends to fit your site.

You can save your own designs too. Select your blocks, open the options menu and choose Create pattern.

When you do, you'll choose whether it's synced:

Synced: every copy stays connected. Edit one and they all update. It's useful for repeated content, like a call to action on many pages.\
Not synced: each copy is independent after it's added. It's useful as a starting layout you'll change each time.

A quick test: if changing it in one place should change it everywhere, make it synced.
