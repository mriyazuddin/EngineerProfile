Everyday blocks stack from top to bottom. Layout blocks let you arrange them side by side and create the sections you see on modern websites.

Group holds several blocks together as a unit. Move a group and everything inside goes with it. Add one background colour or some spacing and it applies to the whole section.

Columns places content side by side, such as three services in a row. On a phone, columns normally stack vertically, so check how it looks on a small screen.

Cover puts text on top of an image, video or colour. It's often used for the big banner at the top of a homepage. Make sure the text stays readable against the background.

The List View button at the top of the editor is useful here. It shows every block in the page and how they're nested, so you can select the right one when blocks are stacked inside others.

You can preview your work in different screen sizes using the editor's preview option.
