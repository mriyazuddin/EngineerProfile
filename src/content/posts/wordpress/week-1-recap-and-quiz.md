Week 1 is done. You now know what WordPress is and what it takes to put a site online, which puts you ahead of many people who've only heard about it.

This week covered:

WordPress is a content management system: you manage a site without writing code.\
It powers a large share of the web, so help is easy to find.\
The software is free, but a domain and hosting usually aren't.\
WordPress.com is a hosted service, and self-hosted runs on hosting you choose.\
A domain name is your site's address, and you renew it to keep it.\
Hosting is where your site's files live.
