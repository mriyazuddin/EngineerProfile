You don't have to finish a post in one sitting, publish it the moment it's ready, or worry about losing an older version. Three built-in features take care of this.

Drafts: click Save draft and your work stays private. Only people with the right access can see it, so come back whenever you like. Use Preview to see how it will look to visitors.

Scheduling: in the post's settings panel, find the publish date, change it to a future date and time, and click Schedule. WordPress publishes it automatically at that time. This is why setting your timezone matters.

Revisions: WordPress saves earlier versions of your content as you work. If you make a mistake, open Revisions in the settings panel, compare versions side by side and restore the one you want.

Together, they make for a calmer workflow: write when you have ideas, review it the next day and schedule it for when your readers are around.

Go deeper: [Revisions guide](https://wordpress.org/documentation/article/revisions/)
