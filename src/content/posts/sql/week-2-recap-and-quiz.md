Filtering is where SQL starts answering real business questions. Let's see how much of this week stuck.

This week covered:

DISTINCT removes repeated values from a result.\
ORDER BY sorts the result, and DESC reverses it.\
WHERE keeps only the rows that match a condition.\
Comparison operators handle equal, not equal, above and below.\
AND, OR and NOT combine conditions.\
IN checks a list, and BETWEEN checks a range.
